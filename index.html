TopoLogic: From Topography to Topology is an experimental spatial research and digital-world archive exploring relationships, flows, and interactions among human and non-human actors beyond conventional geometric representations of space. Developed through a workshop held as part of the 3rd Adana Architecture Festival (15–17 October 2026), curated by Nilüfer Kozikoğlu, the project brings field research, collective mapping, computational exploration, and immersive spatial environments into dialogue.
